# English Abstract

**Title**: Generative AI-Assisted Health Counseling and Monitoring Platform for Weight Management  
**Official Project Website**: **[https://happyhealthhappyheart.com](https://happyhealthhappyheart.com)**  

---

## ABSTRACT

This developmental and applied health informatics study aimed to design and implement a personalized Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI) counseling model for weight management, construct a secure web-based health monitoring platform adhering to the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA), conduct technology transfer workshops, distribute operational manuals, deploy a digital health media hub, and evaluate the clinical health outcomes of an 8-week gamified weight loss intervention. The system infrastructure utilized Laravel Framework 12 as a robust backend service integrated with Inertia.js, React.js, and the LINE Messaging API via Webhook endpoints. The counseling intelligence was powered by the Google Gemini 3.6 Flash Large Language Model, enhanced with a real-time Personalized Context Injection mechanism (dynamically calculating BMR, TDEE, weight trajectories, and physical activity levels) and Multimodal Food Image Recognition for automated caloric estimation, deployed at https://happyhealthhappyheart.com. Following the 8-week intervention among university personnel ($n = 38$), paired t-test analysis demonstrated statistically significant improvements across all physiological body composition parameters ($p < .05$). Specifically, mean body weight significantly decreased by $1.69\text{ kg}$ ($70.46 \pm 20.28$ to $68.77 \pm 19.46\text{ kg}$, $95\%\text{ CI: }1.02\text{ to }2.19$, $t = 6.92$, $p < .001$), Body Mass Index (BMI) significantly declined by $0.63\text{ kg/m}^2$ ($24.84 \pm 3.60$ to $24.21 \pm 3.30\text{ kg/m}^2$, $95\%\text{ CI: }0.41\text{ to }0.85$, $t = 5.96$, $p < .001$), and body fat percentage significantly dropped by $1.36\%$ ($31.59 \pm 7.70\%$ to $30.11 \pm 7.68\%$, $95\%\text{ CI: }1.02\text{ to }1.92\%$, $t = 6.67$, $p < .001$). Crucially, weight reduction occurred without fat-free mass degradation, evidenced by a statistically significant increase in skeletal muscle mass of $0.70\text{ kg}$ ($26.52 \pm 6.77$ to $27.22 \pm 6.88\text{ kg}$, $95\%\text{ CI: }-1.25\text{ to }-0.13$, $t = -2.51$, $p = .017$), confirming healthy caloric deficit execution. Furthermore, participants demonstrated significant gains in health literacy regarding the 3Es (Food, Exercise, Emotion) from $11.26 \pm 2.06$ to $13.45 \pm 1.42$ points ($p < .001$), and overall self-care behaviors from $1.66 \pm 0.42$ to $2.36 \pm 0.17$ points ($p < .001$). Technical benchmarking confirmed an average response latency of $2.4\text{ seconds}$ and $99.8\%$ operational uptime, with overall platform and workshop satisfaction reaching peak levels ($\bar{x} = 4.59 \pm 0.49$ and $\bar{x} = 4.62 \pm 0.50$, respectively). In summary, synergizing Generative AI with mHealth architecture and gamification effectively reinforces health self-regulation and facilitates sustainable physiological weight management.

**Keywords**: Generative AI, Weight Management, Personalized Nutrition, Health Chatbot, Gamification
